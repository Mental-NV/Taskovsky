## How to close a case

Work one bounded state family and one reproducible difference at a time. Record each subcase independently: **reference observed**, **reference unknown**, **Taskovsky verified in this scope**, **gap confirmed**, **candidate**, **integrated**, or **explicitly accepted difference**. A green subset does not close the remaining family.

1. Pin Taskovsky main and tested product revision; record macOS, app version, theme, locale/time zone, viewport in logical points where verified, capture scale, and a neutral disposable fixture. Use a guarded QA build; record any QA-only startup delta and keep product code and user data separate. Identify the linked MAC and TT/UX cases.
2. Observe actual TickTick when accessible. Record exact entry, control/state, interaction, and result. Dated observations remain bounded; match conditions or document why not. Official documentation supplements runtime; static evidence and AX do not establish pixels. Never retry or bypass denied capture or interaction.
3. Verify actual Taskovsky native behavior independently at exact product source, including width boundaries, key/pointer path, persistence/Cancel/Undo as relevant, and neighboring regressions. Compare visual size, position and color separately with calibrated captures and predeclared tolerances/denominators where possible; otherwise record unmeasured axes. Publish only authorized, reviewed images. Do not turn unknown points, visual axes or interaction into passes.
4. Review exact PR head, relevant automated cases and hosted native build/package CI; for docs-only changes verify links and IDs per [workflow](spec/workflow.md#hosted-macos-verification). Link source, scoped reference, independent review, native receipt, and CI beside the affected row; mark integration separately. A family is closed only when its in-scope subcases have evidence or individually documented accepted differences.

## Unknowns and intentional boundaries

- **Reference unknown, not passed:** exact pane breakpoints, compact List fallback behavior and Board interaction, confirmed per-state palettes/tokens, minimum card/icon thresholds, Year scale, exact keyboard and drag semantics, and reference progress/toolbar behavior beyond recorded observations. Capture restrictions are evidence limits, not approved product differences.
- **Documented Taskovsky differences, not hidden passes:** derived pane thresholds; illustrative List/Board previews and selectable Timeline with “preview is not available”; compact formatting-tools palette as an explicit Taskovsky fallback. Credit verified local behavior only. Any new permanent parity exception needs an explicit product decision and scope note.
- **Later verification:** light theme is first. Preserve existing Dark and accessibility behavior; Dark visual parity and dedicated accessibility-layout polish are deferred, not complete. Record keyboard/focus/AX evidence for touched flows now without claiming comprehensive VoiceOver, contrast or reduced-motion acceptance.
- **Excluded from this Mac parity effort:** iPhone parity has its own tracker; no other TickTick modules, account/sync/cloud/sharing, third-party services/external calendars, real OS notifications, storage-recovery polish or App Store work. TaskovskyNet is a separate repository and effort. Rich attachments, tables and full source mode remain undecided in [TT-004](parity-implementation-status.md#tt-004), not silently treated as complete or scheduled work.
