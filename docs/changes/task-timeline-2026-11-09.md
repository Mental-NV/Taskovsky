# Task Timeline

9 November 2026 · TASK-TIMELINE-001

TickTick documents a horizontal project schedule under the Tasks view selector for regular lists, folders, and saved filters. It places dated tasks on a horizontal axis and offers Day, Week, and Month scales. The guide also describes arranging undated tasks, adding on a date, moving tasks, and resizing durations. Smart lists have separate view rules. [TickTick Timeline guide](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055780375439605760).

Taskovsky adds Timeline beside List and Board on an active list. The list's view mode persists with that list. Open a folder from its sidebar menu on Mac or from the arrow next to its disclosure on iPhone. Folders and saved filters offer List and Timeline, and remember that choice for the current window session. The Calendar retains its own modes and source filters.

The date axis offers Day, Week and Month scales, Previous and Next periods, and Today. Task labels stay visible while the axis scrolls. Dated tasks show a point or a duration. Open a row for task details, use its context menu for the precise schedule editor, or use Arrange to schedule an undated task. The schedule editor uses the existing scheduling, recurrence, and Undo rules. The view excludes archived lists and projected recurring copies, and uses the workspace week start. A folder shows its active member lists. A saved filter applies its existing predicates.

### Direct schedule editing

Click a blank date on Mac or press and hold a blank date on iPhone to stage an all-day task on that date. The accessible Add on date action opens a date picker. Capture requires a title and explicit submission. A folder with several active task lists asks which list to use. A capture from a filter can use Inbox or an active list, and existing filter predicates still determine visibility.

Drag a task's scheduled mark to move it. Drag the end grip of a duration to resize it while preserving the start. Day scale snaps timed drags to quarter-hours. All-day drags and Week and Month drags follow civil dates. A visible endpoint and a valid release inside the displayed period are required. The existing recurring scope prompt, reminder policy and schedule Undo apply. Tapping a drag control opens the precise schedule editor.

### Project grouping

A list with sections uses their saved order and shows a No Section group when it contains scheduled tasks. A subtask uses its parent’s section even when the parent is undated or outside the filter. Folder and saved-filter views identify each list and qualify section headings with its name. The labels and chart bands stay aligned when the date axis scrolls. Empty sections are omitted for the current period. Undated tasks stay in Arrange.

### Arrange by dragging

Drag an undated task from Arrange onto the date axis. In Week and Month, the drop creates an all-day point on the selected civil date. In Day, the horizontal position selects a quarter-hour time. The task stays in its list and section. A drop outside the axis leaves it undated, and Undo restores the original schedule. The existing precise schedule editor remains available from the Arrange row.

### Task colors

Use the palette to color scheduled points and bars by list, tag, or priority. The same color rules as Calendar apply, including the list color for a task without a tag. The choice lasts while the current Timeline is open. Changing it leaves tasks and settings unchanged.

### Zoom

Pinch the date axis to change spacing continuously from 75% to 300%. On Mac, Control-scroll also zooms over the date axis. Zoom anchors the date under the gesture when the scroll bounds allow it. The plus, minus, and reset controls use the middle of the visible axis as the anchor. The period and scheduled dates stay the same. Switching Day, Week, or Month returns spacing to 100%. Zoom is local to the current Timeline view.
