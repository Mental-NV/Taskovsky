# Grouped task Boards

The TickTick guide describes Kanban in smart lists, folders, and tags. It shows date columns in Next 7 Days, date or list grouping in Today, and card movement between columns. [TickTick Kanban guide](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782353376903168)

Taskovsky offers grouped Boards for All, Today, Tomorrow, Next 7 Days, Inbox, folders, and tags. Next 7 Days, Tomorrow, and Inbox open grouped by date. Other grouped Boards open by list. Board mode is remembered per destination for the session. Grouping resets when the Board is rebuilt after navigation. Concrete lists keep their own saved Board mode and existing status or section columns.

A folder’s list columns use its active member lists. Smart lists respect each list’s inclusion setting. Date columns use due dates, including the due day of a range. Next 7 Days shows seven days, with a separate Overdue column when applicable. A card can move to a real date or list column. Overdue and No Date are display groups. Date moves preserve all-day civil span or timed duration and due clock, with existing Calendar rules for reminders, recurrence, and Undo. An undated task gets an all-day due date. List moves use existing task and hierarchy rules.

Mac shows scrollable columns with a dedicated grip on cards that can move. Drag the grip onto a highlighted column or use Move to in the card’s context menu. A release outside an eligible column leaves the card in place. iPhone shows one column at a time with tabs, swipes, drag targets, and an explicit Move menu. Saved filters retain their supported List and Timeline modes. Exact reference grouping defaults, custom column widths, and card reordering within a section remain open. Collaboration and external services are deliberately deferred.
