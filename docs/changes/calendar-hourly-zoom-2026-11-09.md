# CAL-HOUR-ZOOM-001 — Mac hourly timeline zoom

In Day, Week and Multi-Day, pinch over the hourly grid or hold Control while scrolling vertically to adjust its scale. Buttons beside the folded-hour controls zoom in and out or reset to 100%. Each button step changes the hourly height by eight points. Gesture zoom is continuous from 32 to 104 points per hour, with a default of 52 points. The all-day strip and date columns keep their sizes.

Zoom holds the wall time under the pointer in place when scrolling bounds permit. The buttons anchor at the center of the visible timeline. Plain scrolling and interaction outside the grid retain their usual behavior. The rulers, fold markers, timed cards, and 15-minute scheduling gestures use the same scale. The scale is temporary and returns to 100% when leaving the hourly views. Workspace and settings files are outside this change.
