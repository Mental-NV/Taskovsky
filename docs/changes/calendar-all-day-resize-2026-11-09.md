# All-day Calendar end resize

9 November 2026 · CAL-ALL-DAY-RESIZE-001

Drag the end handle on an all-day duration to another date in Week, Multi-Day, Month, or Multi-Week. Only the segment containing the actual end shows this handle. The move grip remains separate. The handle previews the selected date and commits when released on a date. Tap or click the handle to open the staged Schedule editor. Day remains a single-date view with access to that editor.

The starting date stays fixed. The selected date becomes the inclusive end and due date. Resizing before the start clamps to a one-day range and keeps its resize handle. A release on the same date produces no change. Future projected occurrences and checklist rows retain their existing read-only controls. Recurring tasks use the existing occurrence or series prompt, and Undo retains its existing schedule-only scope.

TickTick's Week guide documents edge resizing of timed blocks. Taskovsky extends its existing scheduling command to an all-day end handle. The exact reference all-day gesture and edge geometry remain unverified. [TickTick Week guide](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782149730861056#add-duration-tasks).

External Calendar integrations remain deliberately deferred. [Scope decision](../ticktick-spec/taskovsky-parity.md#calendar-integrations-deliberately-deferred--8-november-2026).
