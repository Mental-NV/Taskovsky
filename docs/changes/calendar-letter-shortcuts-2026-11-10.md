# CAL-KEYS-002 — Mac Calendar letter shortcuts

In the full Mac Calendar, D or 1 opens Day, W or 2 opens Week, M or 3 opens Month, Y or 4 opens Year and A or 5 opens Agenda. The view selector shows the two keys for each mode. In the Tasks and Calendar split, the selector continues to work by click and leaves the keys available to Tasks. The keys follow the existing numeric shortcut rules for text focus, sheets, modifiers and repeats. A mode change saves the ordinary Calendar mode preference and leaves task data intact.

On November 10, the installed TickTick 8.2.20 Mac app advertised these five pairs in the Calendar view selector. Unmodified W, Y, D, A and M visibly opened Week, Year, Day, Agenda and Month in that order. The reference returned to the original Month selection and Tasks workspace.
